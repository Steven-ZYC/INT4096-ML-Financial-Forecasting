\section{Neural Networks (Experimental)}
\label{sec:dl}

Outside the main protocol, an MLP on the ten features and an LSTM on their last 20
days predicted next-day direction. We tuned them on the end of the training block
and averaged over \SupSeeds{} seeds (Table~\ref{tab:dl}); the test block was not
used. Training used Adam, batch size 64 and at most \SupMaxEpochs{} epochs,
stopping after \SupPatience{} epochs without hold-out improvement. On AAPL both
scored below ``always up''. On BTC both scored above ``always down'', but all
\SupNCi{} intervals include zero. Hold-out log-loss (\SupLossLo--\SupLossHi) is
close to the coin-flip value $\ln 2 \approx 0.693$. We found no clear evidence
that these networks add predictive signal.
