\section{Neural Networks (Experimental)}
\label{sec:dl}

As an add-on outside the frozen protocol, an MLP on today's ten features and an
LSTM on the last 20 days of them predicted next-day direction, tuned on the end of
the training block and averaged over \SupSeeds{} seeds (Table~\ref{tab:dl}; test
block unused). The networks used Adam, batch size 64 and at most \SupMaxEpochs{}
epochs, stopping after \SupPatience{} epochs without improvement on that hold-out.
Neither beat the majority rule: on AAPL both trailed
``always up'', all \SupNCi{} intervals include zero, and hold-out log-loss
(\SupLossLo--\SupLossHi) is close to the coin-flip value $\ln 2 \approx 0.693$. More
capacity found no signal the classical models missed.
