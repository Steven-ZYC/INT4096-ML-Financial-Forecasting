\section{Challenges and Team Learnings}
\label{sec:learnings}

\textbf{Our most useful moments came from catching our own mistakes.} An
independent AI audit found two high-severity defects, both fixed and retested.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Target representation:} initial runs reported skill near $-1800\%$.
  Without a price level among the features, models collapsed towards the training
  mean. After seeing this, we changed the target to the price change and
  \repofile{docs/research/decision_log.md}{recorded the decision}.
  \item \textbf{Boundary purge:} an auditor found the problem by tracing a label to
  a test price.
  \item \textbf{Testing the tests:} our first leakage tests passed with a one-bar
  forward shift present. A test suite must be shown able to fail.
  \item \textbf{Fairness:} building one shared pipeline took more effort than
  fitting models.
\end{itemize}
