\section{Research Questions}
\label{sec:questions}

\begin{description}[leftmargin=1.4em,style=nextline]
  \item[RQ1.] Under identical, leakage-safe conditions, how do distinct models
  perform against naive persistence on AAPL and BTC?
  \item[RQ2.] How do complexity, tuning and changing market conditions affect
  generalisation and error patterns?
  \item[RQ3.] How much do preprocessing and feature choices change forecast error,
  and does the effect depend on model and market?
  \item[RQ4.] What do examples and counterexamples teach learners about model
  behaviour, evaluation and responsible interpretation?
\end{description}

The protocol states five hypotheses: persistence is competitive at this horizon
(H1); AAPL is more predictable than BTC (H2); scaling moves error more for
scaling-sensitive models than for trees (H3); complexity widens the
train--validation gap (H4); features matter more than the scaler (H5).
