\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We began with a score that looked excellent, then asked what it had beaten. Under
a documented, leakage-audited protocol, \textbf{none of five models meaningfully or
consistently outperformed a one-line persistence rule} on Apple or Bitcoin. The
best skill was \BestAAPLSkill\%, \NBelowAll{} of \NRunTotal{} runs were worse than
the rule, and the experimental networks showed no clear advantage on direction.

The lesson for this course: \textbf{ask of any model what it beat, and how you
know.} The repository lets readers check the claim.
