\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Under a frozen, audited protocol, five machine-learning models failed to beat a
one-line persistence rule on next-session close prediction for Apple and Bitcoin.
The best skill value was \BestAAPLSkill\% and two models were worse than the rule.

For the course's purpose the negative outcome is the contribution: it answers,
concretely and reproducibly, the question we want learners to ask of any model ---
what did you beat, and how do you know? The repository holds the frozen protocol,
immutable data, every run manifest, the audit trail of two real defects and a
notebook that regenerates the analysis, so the claim can be checked rather than
believed. Nothing here is investment advice, and no claim is made that the lesson improved
anyone's understanding --- only that it makes these ideas testable by the next
person who runs it.
