\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Under a frozen, audited protocol, five machine-learning models failed to beat a
one-line persistence rule on next-session close prediction for Apple and Bitcoin.
The best skill value was \BestAAPLSkill\%, \NBelowAll{} of \NRunTotal{} runs were worse than the
rule, and an experimental MLP and LSTM did no better.

For the course's purpose the negative outcome is the contribution: it answers,
reproducibly, the question we want learners to ask of any model --- what did you
beat, and how do you know? The repository lets the claim be checked rather than
believed. Nothing here is investment advice, and no claim is made that the lesson
improved anyone's understanding.
