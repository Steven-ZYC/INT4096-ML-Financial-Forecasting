# Feature Dictionary

Generated by `python -m int4096.data.cards` from `int4096.features.build`.

Every feature is computable from bars `<= t`. The prediction is made
immediately after bar `t` closes.

| Feature | Definition | Trailing window |
|---|---|---|
| `ret_1` | Close_t / Close_{t-1} - 1 | 1 |
| `ret_5` | Close_t / Close_{t-5} - 1 | 5 |
| `ret_20` | Close_t / Close_{t-20} - 1 | 20 |
| `ma_gap_5` | Close_t / SMA_5,t - 1 (trailing mean) | 5 |
| `ma_gap_20` | Close_t / SMA_20,t - 1 (trailing mean) | 20 |
| `vol_10` | trailing std of 1-bar returns | 10 |
| `vol_30` | trailing std of 1-bar returns | 30 |
| `range_1` | (High_t - Low_t) / Close_t | 1 |
| `vol_chg` | Volume_t / SMA_5(Volume)_t - 1 | 5 |
| `dow` | day of week, 0=Monday | 0 |

## Explicit prohibitions

- No centred rolling window (`center=True`).
- No negative shift inside the design matrix.
- No `bfill` / backward fill.
- No whole-dataset statistic in feature construction or preprocessing.
- No price-level feature, so the label must be modelled as a change (see protocol decision D8).

These are enforced by `tests/test_temporal_integrity.py`.
